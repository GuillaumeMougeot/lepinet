On the trap images the split is made by capture group: a random 10\,\% of the (trap, night) groups calibrates the thresholds and the rest is scored, so that no moth photographed on one night appears on both sides.
On the probe set this changes calibrated macro-$\text{F}_1$ by 1.0 point on average (between $-0.7$ and $+2.4$ over 92 models) and no comparison between heads; the only pairs of models that change order are less than 1.7 points apart.
