Two sources of variation are reported separately.
Retraining with another seed measures how much a score depends on the training run; we train every configuration with three seeds (42, 43, 44) and report the range.
On the test fold it is negligible: across twelve configurations (three backbones, four heads) the species macro-$\text{F}_1$ varies by a median of 0.07 points and at most 0.14.
On the trap images it is not: the calibrated species score varies by a median of 1.2 points and up to 2.7, and on the held-out-species probe by up to 5.1.
Calibration contributes to this, since the same configurations vary by only 0.2 to 0.5 points at zero threshold.
Bootstrap resampling of whole (trap, night) groups measures how much a score depends on which capture events were drawn, and on the trap images it is of the same size as retraining: a standard deviation of 0.25 to 0.30 points at zero threshold, against a three-seed range of 0.19 to 0.50.\footnote{Percentile intervals of this bootstrap sit above the point estimate, because a resample drops rare species that are only ever predicted and score zero; we therefore quote its standard deviation.}
A difference between two configurations is claimed only when their three-seed ranges do not overlap, and on the trap images only when it also exceeds twice the bootstrap standard deviation.
