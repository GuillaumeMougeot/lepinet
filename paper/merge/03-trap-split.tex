Because the trap images serve both as the benchmark and as the only source of unlabelled target-domain images (\cref{sec:selftraining}), they are partitioned by capture group, a pair of trap and night with nights running from 23:00 to 04:00 CET.
Groups are assigned at random to an adaptation set (27,230 crops of 383 species), a probe set (15,200 crops of 368 species) that no training run touches, and an unused remainder (5,475 crops of 72 species).
Fifteen percent of the species (75) are withheld from the adaptation set to form the held-out-species subset (2,455 crops). 
The held-out-species subset separates adaptation to the capture conditions from specialisation on the species that were adapted to.
Only 58 of these 75 species can be found in the probe set.
The unused remainder is the crops of the withheld species that fell on adaptation nights.
Grouping by night is necessary: 35\,\% of crops have a near pixel-identical twin elsewhere in the same night.
