Global Lepidoptera is assembled from GBIF occurrence images of adult moths and butterflies \autocite{gbif}, capped at about 2,000 images per species when the corpus was built.
After quality filtering it holds 6.33\,M images of 12,632 species, 4,476 genera and 104 families.
The images are split into ten folds of equal size: fold 0 is the test set, fold 1 the validation set and folds 2--9 the training set.
Unless stated otherwise, results trained with the lepinet recipe (\cref{sec:training}) also drop species with fewer than 50 training images.
This leaves 12,041 species, 4,333 genera and 102 families, 5.04\,M training images and a test fold of 629,742 images.
The species below the floor are never trained on, and supply the unseen species of the open-set benchmark (\cref{sec:evaluation}).
