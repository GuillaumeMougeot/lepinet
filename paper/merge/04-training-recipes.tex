\subsection{Training}\label{sec:training}
All models are trained with one recipe, implemented in mini\_trainer, whose settings were fixed by single-factor comparisons before the experiments reported here.
Every species with a training image is a class (12,632 for Lepidoptera, no minimum count), sampling is uniform over images and the loss is cross-entropy, so the recipe applies no class rebalancing.
The whole network is optimised with AdamW (learning rate $10^{-3}$, weight decay 0.01, batches of 64) under a cosine schedule that warms up linearly over half an epoch from 1\,\% of the learning rate and anneals to zero.
Images are read at 460 pixels, augmented by flips, rotations up to $15^\circ$ and zoom up to 1.1 (applied with probability 0.75), and cropped to 256 pixels; Lepidoptera models train for 5 epochs and Pl@ntNet models for 10 epochs at 384 pixels.
The classifier is a cosine head with a trainable scale and bias, kept in fp32 while the rest of the network trains in fp16 mixed precision.
Every configuration is trained with three seeds (42, 43, 44).
Two backbones need their own optimiser settings: ViT-L/16 trains with a learning rate of $10^{-4}$, and the DINOv3 ConvNeXt-L with Muon at $8\times10^{-4}$ on 320-pixel crops ($2\times10^{-4}$ on Pl@ntNet).

The recipe deliberately leaves two choices to the dataset, each measured in the results: class rebalancing, for training sets with a heavy tail (\cref{sec:results_longtail}), and unlabelled images of the target domain, when the deployment domain differs from the training photographs (\cref{sec:results_selftraining}).
