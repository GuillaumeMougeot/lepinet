\section{Nameable nuisance}\label{appendix:nuisance}
Adding motion blur, low light and JPEG quantisation to training raises species macro-$\text{F}_1$ on the 47,905 trap crops from 0.331 (mean of three seeds without it) to 0.361, the probe set from 0.366 to 0.405 and its held-out species from 0.301 to 0.321, for 0.69 points on the test fold and at no inference cost (BU on EfficientNetV2-S, one seed).
Each transform encodes a guess about what differs between the domains, so the gain measures the part of the gap that can be named in advance; it is small next to self-training on trap images (\cref{sec:results_selftraining}).

\section{Adapting only the classifier}\label{appendix:classifier}
Re-training only the classifier of a frozen backbone with square-root oversampling (cRT, \textbf{(REF: Kang et al. 2020)}) raises the test fold by 1.1 points, and training only the classifier on the pseudo-labelled trap crops raises the probe set to 0.509; adapting the whole network for the same two epochs gives 0.619.
Because the whole network adapts better, the results use end-to-end self-training (\cref{sec:results_selftraining}).
