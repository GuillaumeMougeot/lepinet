\subsection{Which open-set scoring rule}\label{sec:results_rules}
The five rules of \cref{sec:evaluation} are computed from the same forward pass on the same images.
With the recipe's cosine head their order is the same for every head on both backbones: entropy, then MSP, then the top-2 margin, then the largest logit, then energy.
For the flat head on EfficientNetV2-S, entropy gives 0.915 and energy 0.599, a 31.6-point spread, and the largest logit, a common default, is 24 to 38 points below MSP at 20\,M parameters and 8 to 31 points below it at 198\,M.
In a well-fitted cosine embedding every image reaches a high cosine to some prototype, so the maximum saturates; what still separates known from novel images is whether one prototype dominates, which only MSP, entropy and the top-2 margin see.
The choice of rule also decides what capacity appears to buy: from 20\,M to 198\,M parameters the flat head gains 1.0 point read by entropy and 17.6 read by the largest logit.
On trap images the rules that read the size of the logits fail: the largest logit and energy fall below chance for seven of the eight heads (0.295 and 0.272 for the flat head on EfficientNetV2-S), because shift lowers every logit, known species included.
The rules that read the shape of the softmax keep working, and on trap images MSP, entropy and the top-2 margin are within 3 points of each other for seven of eight heads; every rule loses most of its power there (best AUROC 0.54 to 0.66, against 0.87 to 0.93 on museum-style images).
Open-set results are therefore reported with the rule named, and the size of the logits is never used as a novelty score.
