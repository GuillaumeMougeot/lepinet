\subsection{Which classes are averaged changes the size of an effect}\label{sec:results_conventions}
On the test fold every species occurs in the ground truth and none is predicted without occurring, so averaging over the species present and over the species present or predicted (\cref{sec:metrics}) give identical scores.
On the probe set, which holds 368 of the 12,632 species, they do not (\cref{tab:conventions}).

\begin{table}[htb]
    \centering
    \caption{Probe-set species macro-$\text{F}_1$ under three conventions. Present only: the average runs over species in the ground truth. Present or predicted: species that are predicted but absent count with $\text{F}_1 = 0$, at zero threshold and calibrated on 10\,\% of the (trap, night) groups. Three-seed means where marked ($^\ast$).}
    \label{tab:conventions}
    \begin{tabular}{lccc}
        \toprule
        model & present only & present or predicted & calibrated \\
        \midrule
        EfficientNetV2-S, BU, no trap data$^\ast$ & 0.648 & 0.168 & 0.366 \\
        \quad + pseudo-labels, 2.5\,\% & 0.717 & 0.381 & 0.593 \\
        \quad + true labels, confident crops & 0.751 & 0.566 & 0.682 \\
        \quad + true labels, every species & 0.777 & 0.696 & 0.722 \\
        DINOv3 ConvNeXt-L, BU, no trap data$^\ast$ & 0.694 & 0.189 & 0.457 \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Counting predicted-only species triples the gain from self-training (6.9 points present only, 21.3 at zero threshold, 22.7 calibrated) and multiplies the lead of true labels over self-training fivefold at zero threshold (6.0 against 31.5 points).
Restricting a model to the 464 species recorded at the traps changes present-only scores of two fine-tuned BioCLIP-2 models by $-2.0$ and $+0.4$ points and gains 28 to 30 points at zero threshold and 15 to 16 calibrated.
Both labels and checklists confine predictions to the local fauna.
When only present species are averaged, a wrong answer outside the probe species lowers one species' recall and costs nothing else; when predicted species count, every absent species named adds a class with $\text{F}_1 = 0$, and confining predictions removes them.
Comparisons between models with the same label space keep their direction under every convention: every model in \cref{tab:conventions} ranks in the same order under all three.
A macro-$\text{F}_1$ on a subset of the label space is therefore uninterpretable unless the class set is stated.
