\subsection{Accuracy on the test fold does not predict accuracy under shift}\label{sec:results_axes}
\Cref{tab:axes} scores the models of the shift experiments on every axis.
The eight EfficientNetV2-S models lie within 0.9 points on the test fold and span 35.6 points on the trap probe set and 9.1 on its held-out species.
Self-training, worth 22.2 points on the probe set (\cref{sec:results_selftraining}), changes the test fold by 0.07.
Across the ten models the test-fold ranking is unrelated to the probe ranking (Spearman $\rho = -0.04$) and only loosely related to the held-out-species ranking ($\rho = 0.54$, not significant).
The axes also pick different models: true trap labels over every species give the best model on the probe set, which is eighth of ten on the test fold and seventh on held-out species, while the self-trained DINOv3 ConvNeXt-L is best on the test fold and on held-out species and third on the probe set, 9.5 points below the best.
