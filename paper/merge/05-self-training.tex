\subsection{Self-training on unlabelled target images}\label{sec:selftraining}
A model trained with the same recipe but without trap images (the teacher) predicts a species for every crop of the adaptation set.
The most confident 30\,\% of these predictions are kept (8,169 crops of 274 species, confidence above 0.953).
The expert labels of the adaptation set are never read when selecting them; measured against those labels, 98.7\,\% of the kept predictions are correct, against 69.1\,\% for all adaptation crops.
The pseudo-labelled crops are added to the training set of Global Lepidoptera and a new model is trained from scratch with the recipe; they are never used for validation or model selection.
The dose is the share of training rows that are pseudo-labelled: each crop is repeated so that the pseudo-labels make up a chosen share, 2.5\,\% (16 copies) unless stated, and the results vary this share (\cref{sec:results_selftraining}).
