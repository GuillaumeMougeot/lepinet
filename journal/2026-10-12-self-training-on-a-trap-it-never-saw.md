# Does self-training help at a trap, or a site, it never saw? (X1, X2)

**Kind:** research · **Status:** **OPEN (2026-10-13)**: X2 complete, X1 7 of 12 folds in (OH4,
SS1-4 running). So far: **accuracy transfers, macro-F1 mostly does not.** A held-out trap or site
keeps 79-99 % of the accuracy gain but only 23-56 % of the macro-F1 gain. H2 is falsified, H1 is
heading that way.

## The question (the owner's, 2026-10-12)

B3rep5x's +7.94 on the probe set holds out *nights*: adapt and probe share all 12 traps and both
summers ([[2026-10-12-do-trap-moths-leak-across-nights]]). So it measures adaptation to capture
conditions at traps the model has already seen. A deployment adds new traps, often at new sites.
How much of the gain survives when the trap being scored contributed no images at all?

## Design

The 12 traps are 3 sites of 4 (LV, OH, SS). That nesting decides what "leave one out" means:

| split | held out | what it tests |
|---|---|---|
| probe (B3rep5x, done) | nights | new nights at a known trap |
| **X1, leave-one-trap-out** (12 folds) | one trap | a new trap, with its three neighbours at the same site in training |
| **X2, leave-one-site-out** (3 folds) | one site's four traps | a new site |

The owner asked for X1. X2 was added because X1 alone can look like site transfer while the site's
other three traps are still in training.

**One factor per run.** Each fold is B3rep5x exactly, with the held-out traps' rows removed from
`pseudo_strict.parquet`: the same pseudo-labels, the same gate, the same config, the same ~2.6 %
share of training rows (`--target-frac 0.02`; replication rises from 11x to 12-13x to keep the
share as images are removed; dry run LV1: 10,741 rows, 12x; site-OH: 10,159 rows, 13x).
`dev/089_trap_holdout.py` builds each fold inside its job.

**A confound the site folds carry (seen in the first job's log).** LV holds 54 % of the
pseudo-labels, so site-LV keeps 5,634 images at **23x** to stay at the same share. The replication
sweep showed that heavier replication of fewer images moves the gain from transfer toward
memorisation (27x was worse than 11x at a higher share). So a loss on site-LV is "new site"
*plus* "half the unique target images, each seen twice as often". The trap folds (12-13x) and
site-OH and site-SS (smaller sites) are much less affected. Read site-LV with that in mind; if it
is the outlier, the control is site-LV at 11x (a ~1.2 % share), one more run.

**Scoring on identical crops.** Each fold model scores the whole probe set. `dev/089 pool` takes,
for every probe crop, the prediction of the fold that never saw its trap (X1) or site (X2): one
macro-F1 over the same 15,200 crops B1 and B3rep5x were scored on, plus the held-out-species subset
and a per-trap breakdown. Every column is recomputed with one function. On probe that function reads
B1 0.6923 / B3rep5x 0.7719 (official 0.6912 / 0.7706, a label-set convention). On held-out species
it matches the official numbers exactly (0.6974 / 0.7704).

**Noise.** One end-to-end 20 M run has a 0.0041 probe floor. A pooled score averages 12 (or 3)
independent runs over disjoint crops, so its run-to-run spread should be no larger; the per-trap
cells are single runs on 393-2,202 crops and are noisier.

## Hypotheses (committed 2026-10-12, before any result)

* **H1. X1 keeps most of the gain:** pooled probe **0.755-0.770**, against 0.7706 for held-out
  nights and 0.6912 with no trap data. Traps at one site share hardware, sheet, light and fauna, and
  T2 found 83 % of the adaptation sits in the classifier, i.e. in which species and appearances to
  expect. Neighbours supply both.
* **H2. X2 loses more but keeps more than half:** pooled probe **0.730-0.760**. A new site changes
  the species mix and possibly the hardware. But the gap self-training closes is mostly museum
  photo → trap photo, which every site shares.
* **H3. The ordering B1 < X2 <= X1 <= B3rep5x holds in most per-trap cells**, with the largest X2
  losses at the site whose fauna differs most. Per-trap B1 ranges from 0.59 (SS3) to 0.76 (SS1).
* **H4. Held-out species gain about as much as the rest.** Those species were never pseudo-labelled
  in any split, so what they receive is the condition adaptation, which should not depend on the
  trap.

**What would change the paper.** If X1 lands below 0.745, a large share of the gain was
trap-specific (memorising a sheet, a camera angle, a local species list). §4.11 would then have to
say "adapts to the traps it was given" rather than "to trap conditions".

## Runs

15 jobs, `ucloud/holdout/lepinet-holdout-<fold>.toml` (generated by `dev/089 specs`), B200 x 1,
about 10 h each, about 150 GPU-hours, no CPU core-hours. Each job trains, scores probe, and deletes
its 0.5 GB combined parquet and preprocessing cache. Predictions:
`data/ucloud_preds/holdout-<fold>-probe/`.

## Result (interim, 2026-10-13)

**X2, leave-one-site-out, complete** (15,200 probe crops, pooled; recomputed macro-F1, so B1 and
B3rep5x read 0.6923 / 0.7719 here):

| | probe | held-out species |
|---|---|---|
| B1, no trap data | 0.6923 | 0.6974 |
| **held-out site (X2)** | **0.7105** | **0.7363** |
| B3rep5x, held-out night | 0.7719 | 0.7704 |

| site | pseudo-labels (fold keeps) | crops | B1 | X2 | B3rep5x | macro-F1 gain kept | accuracy gain kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LV | 6,596 (5,634, 23x) | 7,749 | 0.6898 | 0.7179 | 0.7833 | 30 % | 97 % |
| OH | 2,071 (10,159, 13x) | 2,771 | 0.6824 | 0.7200 | 0.7605 | 48 % | 90 % |
| SS | 3,563 (8,667) | 4,680 | 0.7039 | 0.7273 | 0.7519 | 49 % | 79 % |

**X1, leave-one-trap-out, interim** (folds LV1-4 and OH1-3: 9,743 crops):

| | probe | held-out species | accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | 0.6842 | 0.6590 | 0.648 |
| X2, held-out site | 0.7017 | 0.7095 | 0.824 |
| **X1, held-out trap** | **0.7322** | **0.6935** | **0.831** |
| B3rep5x | 0.7699 | 0.7386 | 0.833 |

**Where the macro-F1 goes.** Probe crops are split by whether their species is pseudo-labelled at
some *other* trap, so that the held-out-trap fold still sees it. The cells are macro recall over
species, on the 9,743 X1 crops:

| species in the crop | crops / species | B1 | B3rep5x | X1 | X2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pseudo-labelled at other traps | 7,683 / 199 | 0.709 | 0.859 | **0.855** | 0.821 |
| pseudo-labelled only at this trap | 134 / 27 | 0.761 | 0.890 | 0.774 | 0.774 |
| never pseudo-labelled | 1,895 / 101 | 0.529 | 0.613 | 0.554 | 0.554 |

* **Common, widespread species carry the accuracy, and for them nothing is lost.** A species
  pseudo-labelled at a neighbouring trap is recognised at the held-out trap as well as at its own:
  0.855 vs 0.859.
* **The macro-F1 gain lives in the tail, and the tail is local.** Species pseudo-labelled only at
  the held-out trap lose most of their gain. The surprise is species *never* pseudo-labelled
  anywhere: B3rep5x lifts them +8.4 and the held-out-trap fold keeps only +2.5. Self-training's
  help to species it never labelled, the "condition adaptation" of §4.11, is largely specific to
  the trap whose images it trained on: the sheet, the camera, the light. It is not a general
  museum → trap shift.
* **Errors spread.** The fold models scatter their errors over more species (464 distinct wrong
  labels vs 400), and more of them go to species never recorded at that trap (57 % vs 50 %).

**Hypotheses so far.**

* H1 (X1 pooled 0.755-0.770): interim **0.7322**, i.e. 56 % of the gain. Below the 0.745 line set
  for rewording §4.11. Falsified unless the five SS/OH4 folds land far above the rest.
* H2 (X2 0.730-0.760): **0.7105, falsified**. A new site keeps 23 % of the macro-F1 gain.
* H3 (B1 < X2 <= X1 <= B3rep5x per trap): holds pooled. Per trap it holds in 6 of 7 cells. LV1 is
  the exception: X1 and X2 both beat B3rep5x there (0.80 / 0.78 vs 0.78). Per-trap cells score
  only the species present at that trap, so they ignore false positives onto absent species and
  read higher than pooled.
* H4 (held-out species gain as much as the rest): mixed. Under X2 held-out species keep 53 % of
  their gain, more than the 23 % overall. Under X1 interim they keep 43 %.
* The site-LV dose confound (23x) did not make LV an outlier in accuracy (97 % kept). Its
  macro-F1 retention (30 %) is the lowest of the three sites, but not by enough to justify the
  11x control yet.

**What it changes, pending the last five folds.** §4.11's "adapts to capture conditions" needs a
qualifier. Self-training on a trap's own unlabelled images adapts the model to *that trap*. The
widespread species transfer to a new trap; the gain on rare and unlabelled species mostly does not.
Operationally that is good news, because the recipe needs no labels and a new trap's own images
are free: run it per deployment. It is not a model one adapts once and ships to new sites.
