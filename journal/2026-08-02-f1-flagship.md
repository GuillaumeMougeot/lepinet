# F1: marginal supervision's robustness benefit does not survive scale — but its calibration benefit does

**Kind:** research · **Status:** **RESOLVED (2026-08-03), NARROWED same day.** The conclusion below
is measured on the **full** trap set. On the `probe` benchmark F1 beats B4 by **+2.03 pt** (5x its
floor) and on held-out trap species by **+4.58** — so marginal supervision's robustness benefit
*does* survive scale there. The two benchmarks weight different species sets and macro-F1 does not
decompose across them: [[2026-08-03-macro-f1-does-not-decompose]]. **Prediction falsified**, at the line written
before the run. Composing all four wins at 198 M gives **exactly B4** on species and on shift; the
+1.79 pt shifted gain marginal supervision bought at 20 M is worth **+0.02 pt** here, thirty times
below the noise floor. What *does* survive is the coarse-level gain: genus **+0.40**, family
**+0.74**, both several times their floors. Two mechanisms that looked like one, separated by scale.

## The run

Everything that had won, composed for the first time: DINOv3-ConvNeXt-L, ArcFace × z-score, marginal
supervision, `domain_aug: trap`. No coarse parameters.

**Committed prediction:** in-distribution 0.918–0.925, shifted **0.720–0.745**, open-set 0.88–0.90,
*"falsified if shifted ≤ 0.717 — that would mean marginal supervision's robustness benefit does not
survive scale, which would be the single most surprising result of the project so far."*

| | in-distribution | shifted | open-set |
|---|---|---|---|
| predicted | 0.918–0.925 | **0.720–0.745** | 0.88–0.90 |
| **actual** | **0.9219** ✓ | **0.7103** ✗ | **0.8800** ✓ (entropy rule) |

**Falsified**, and by the exact criterion set in advance. In-distribution and open-set landed inside
their ranges; shifted landed below the floor.

## What this actually says

At 20 M, marginal supervision did two things. It improved the coarse levels
([[2026-07-30-marginal-supervision]]: +0.27 genus, +0.39 family) *and* it recovered 1.41–1.79 pt
under domain shift. Those were read as one effect. **They are not.**

**The coarse gain is a direct effect and it scales.** Supervising the marginals optimises exactly the
quantity the coarse levels are read from, so it works wherever it is applied — and at 198 M it is in
fact *larger* than at 20 M (+0.40/+0.74 vs +0.27/+0.39), presumably because a better embedding has
more calibration left to extract.

**The robustness gain was a regularisation effect, and regularisation benefits vanish with
capacity.** The auxiliary coarse losses constrain a model that does not have enough capacity to
satisfy the species objective cleanly; a 198 M model does, so the extra signal buys nothing it was
not already getting.

That reading is not invented for this result — **it is the third time the same pattern appears at
this scale boundary**:

| intervention | at 20 M | at 198 M |
|---|---|---|
| domain augmentation's in-distribution *cost* | −0.36 | **0.00** ([[2026-08-01-capacity-x-augmentation]]) |
| marginal supervision's shifted *benefit* | +1.79 | **+0.02** |
| marginal supervision's coarse benefit | +0.27/+0.39 | +0.40/+0.74 (persists) |

Capacity absorbs the things that act as constraints, and leaves untouched the things that act
directly on a quantity being measured. **A generalisable warning: an intervention validated on a
small model may be buying regularisation, and regularisation does not transfer upward.** This project
has now tested five interventions at both scales and three behaved differently.

## Consequences

**B4 and F1 are interchangeable for deployment**, so pick on the coarse levels: F1 is better at genus
and family for no species or shift cost, at the price of ~0.9 pt of open-set AUROC. If the app shows
a genus when it cannot resolve the species — which it does — **F1 is the one to ship.**

**The recommended architecture is unchanged but its justification narrows.** "Single head + ArcFace ×
z-score + marginal supervision" stands; the claim attached to marginal supervision must become *"it
buys coarse calibration at any scale, and robustness only below ~100 M parameters."*

**A6-style controls are now mandatory across the scale boundary too.** Every intervention this
project adopted was validated at 20 M. Three of five did not transfer. The others —
√-oversampling, the ArcFace margin — have never been re-tested at 198 M in isolation, and on this
evidence should not be assumed to behave the same way.
